Privacy Policy

Valmiki Ramayana (Android app, package app.valmiki.katha)
Effective 15 November 2026

In one sentence

The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information about you.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account and no sign-in. It never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It shows no advertisements, contains no advertising software and does not use the advertising ID.
  • It contains no analytics and no crash-reporting service. We do not see how you use the app.
  • The app's own code does not contact any server of ours. We do not run one.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps a few settings on your phone so that it works the way you left it: your language, the Gen Z switch, whether the Sanskrit verse is shown, the playback speed, the last sarga you opened and where you stopped in each sarga. It also keeps the narration you chose to download. None of this leaves your phone, and it is not included in Android's cloud backup. Removing the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes all of it.

Downloads through Google Play

The app and each Kanda's narration are delivered by Google Play. When you tap Download, Google Play fetches that narration pack for you. To do this, Google's Play delivery software, which is part of the app, sends Google technical information: device metadata (such as the device type) and the app version. This is the only information that leaves your phone through the app. Google states that it is used to deliver the right pack, is encrypted, is not passed to third parties, and is deleted after a fixed retention period. Google handles it under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the playback controls and download progress. You can refuse; the app still works.
  • Internet and network state: for the downloads through Google Play described above.
  • Run in the background (foreground service, wake lock, start after restart): so that narration keeps playing with the screen off and an unfinished download can finish.

Children

The app is intended for listeners aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a later version were to carry advertising, the new text and its date will be shown on this page before that version is released.
